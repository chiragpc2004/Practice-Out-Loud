The problem

India talks to its phone. Just not to ChatGPT.

Indians use voice search at twice the global rate and send voice notes daily. Yet only 15% of job seekers we surveyed use ChatGPT voice weekly. The barrier isn't awareness. It's context and control.

65%say people around them stop them from speaking
53%won't use voice because they can't review before it sends
44%worry it won't understand their accent or Hinglish
68%would try voice for mock interviews

"My roommate is right there. I'm not saying 'tell me about yourself' to my phone."Participant 1 · final-year B.Tech student, Bengaluru

The solution

Give voice a job it does better than typing

Practice Out Loud offers a short mock interview right when someone is preparing for one, and removes the reasons they'd hesitate to speak.

01 · Discover

A nudge at the moment of need

A practice card appears after a job-prep message, not as a generic mic icon.

02 · Set up

Privacy made visible

Context is pre-filled. Choose English, Hinglish or Hindi. Quiet mode turns on with earphones.

03 · Answer

Hold to talk

One question at a time, with a WhatsApp-style gesture. "Type instead" is always one tap away.

04 · Review

Nothing is scored unchecked

An editable transcript, with unsure words flagged, before anything counts.

05 · Feedback

A reason to speak

Scores on structure, clarity and filler words, plus a stronger rewrite to say again.

06 · Return

From session to habit

The summary is saved to chat, with an opt-in reminder before the interview.
