So what

Voice wins in India when it is vernacular, code-mixed and low-stakes.

India is now ChatGPT's largest market by weekly users, doubled since Jan 2026 [7] — but skews to English-comfortable students (18–24s send ~half of messages) [8].

The next wave will speak before it types.
