Verdicts

Confirmed: H1 shared spaces, H3 control, H2 Hinglish · Partly: H4 value, H5 speed · Rejected: H6 privacy

Primary research: survey of 34 job seekers and freshers who use the ChatGPT mobile app (multi-select, up to 3 barriers), plus six 1:1 interviews with a live voice test.

Top barriers are social (65%) and control (53%), with Hinglish accuracy (44%) amplifying both. Privacy is not a real blocker. Demand exists: 68% would try voice for mock interviews.
