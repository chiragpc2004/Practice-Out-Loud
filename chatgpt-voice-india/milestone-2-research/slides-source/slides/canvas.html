Built from the survey (N = 34) and 6 interviews

The barrier isn't the mic — it's where and how job seekers prep

Who

Job seekers and freshers (21–30) who use ChatGPT mobile weekly to prep for jobs

What

They type every prompt, even mock-interview practice; only 15% use voice weekly

Where & when

Late-night or commute prep in PGs, shared rooms and offices — 82% rarely alone

Why it matters

Only 32% feel interview-ready; spoken practice builds confidence, and ChatGPT misses its best voice use case

Current workaround

Type answers and read them silently (5 of 6); rehearse aloud only when alone or with a friend

Root cause

Awkward around others (65%), no review before high-stakes prompts (53%); Hinglish errors amplify (44%)

Problem statement

Job seekers in India don't use ChatGPT voice because they prep in shared spaces and need precise, editable prompts. Voice feels awkward and risky, even for spoken interview practice, where it would help most.

HMW help job seekers practise with ChatGPT by voice, even around others, with the control their high-stakes prompts need?

Source for 32%: Apna Interview Readiness Index 2025 (via Business Today). All other figures: this study's survey (N = 34) and interviews.
