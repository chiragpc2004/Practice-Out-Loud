Why voice matters, and a KPI tree to grow it

Voice is ChatGPT's bridge to India's next 100M users

Reach

~90% of new internet users will prefer regional languages [14]; voice skips Indic-script typing.

Depth

Speaking is 3.7× faster in Indic languages [3] → longer, richer prompts → better answers.

Defence

Gemini (118M MAU vs ChatGPT's 180M, Jan 2026 [15]) leads with voice in 25+ Indian languages [12].

Habit

Voice fits commute, cooking and homework moments — sessions beyond the desk.

Product outcome

Make voice a default way Indians ask ChatGPT → more weekly users, more use per user, better Tier 2/3 retention.

North Star: weekly voice messages from India

= Weekly active voice users × voice messages per voice user

Weekly active voice users

Awareness · % WAU shown a voice entry or nudge

Trial · % who tap mic and send a first voice prompt

First-use success · sent without edit, retry or cancel

Repeat · 2nd voice use within 7 days

Voice messages per voice user

Frequency · voice sessions per user per week

Depth · voice turns per session

Accuracy · word error rate on Hinglish & Indic speech

Speed · latency to first response

Guardrails

Voice abandonment · retype-after-voice · voice 1★ reviews · cost per voice minute
